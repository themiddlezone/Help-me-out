## Devices

### Which phones are supported?

Only the **Samsung Galaxy A12 SM-A127F**, the Exynos 850 model. More devices may come later, and they will show up on
the [downloads page](../) when they do.

### Why does the site say a12s when my phone is a Galaxy A12?

`a12s` is the codename of the SM-A127F. Android device trees, build files and ROM file names all use codenames rather
than marketing names, so that is what you will see in file names like `lineage-23.2-...-a12s-rom38.zip`.

The codename also keeps it apart from the other Galaxy A12. Samsung sold two very different phones under that name,
and only the SM-A127F is the Exynos one these builds are for.

### Will it work on the SM-A125F?

No. The SM-A125F uses a MediaTek chip, so it needs completely different software. Do not flash these files on it.

### How do I check my model?

Open **Settings**, then **About phone**, and read **Model number**. Download mode also prints it as the product name
at the top of the screen.

## Builds

### What is the difference between Official and Test builds?

Official builds are the recommended releases. Test builds are GitHub pre-releases: they carry the newest changes and
are there for people happy to find bugs. Each build page shows which one it is.

### What works and what does not?

Every release lists this in its notes, under **What works** and **Known issues**. Open a build from the
[ROM downloads](../a12s/roms/) to see it.

### Does LineageOS come with Google apps?

No. LineageOS ships without them. The release notes do not cover any Google apps package yet, so ask on
[Telegram](https://t.me/Varifocal) before trying one.

### Why does the Updater app say there are no updates?

These are unofficial builds and are not on LineageOS's update servers. Follow [Updating](../docs/a12s/updating/)
instead.

### How do I know a download is not corrupted?

Every file on a build page has its SHA-256 checksum next to it. Run `sha256sum <file>` on Linux or macOS, or
`certutil -hashfile <file> SHA256` on Windows, and compare the result before flashing.

## Safety

### Will this void my warranty?

Unlocking the bootloader trips Samsung's Knox flag, which cannot be reset. Samsung may refuse warranty repairs on a
phone with a tripped flag, and Knox features like Secure Folder and Samsung Wallet stop working for good.

### Will my banking apps still work?

Maybe not. Apps that check device integrity can refuse to run on a phone with an unlocked bootloader. Check the
apps you depend on before you flash.

### Is my data encrypted?

It depends on the build. Every release lists **SELinux** and **Userdata** in its notes. Release 1 leaves user data
unencrypted and runs SELinux in permissive mode, while newer test builds encrypt user data and enforce SELinux. On an
unencrypted build, keep a screen lock on and keep sensitive data off the phone.

  LineageOS.

### How do I go back to Samsung's software?

Download the stock firmware for the SM-A127F in your region and flash it with Odin. Using the `CSC` file, not
`HOME_CSC`, gives you a clean start. Only relock the bootloader once stock has booted and is working.

## This site

### What does the Motion button in the footer do?

It sets how much the site animates. **Full** turns on every spring and page transition. **Lite** turns them off,
which helps on slow phones. **Auto** picks for you based on your system's reduced motion setting and how well your
device keeps up.
